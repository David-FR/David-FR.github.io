01 · The problem

Every query costs. Every pixel shows.

A patch can fool an object detector, but its practical cost depends on how many times the attacker queries the model and how much of the image the patch covers.

02 · The approach

Adapt three decisions together

PatchBandit searches for a useful location, improves the pattern, and increases its size only after progress stalls, all through a score-based black-box interface.

03 · The finding

Detector architecture changes the trade-off

The baseline suppresses the original top class in 77.5–89.7% of tested cases on the two CNN detectors and 59.1% on the transformer detector.
