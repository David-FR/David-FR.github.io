On YOLOv5, earlier growth roughly halves queries

GP5 shortens the patience before increasing patch size. It retains almost the same success rate and median footprint as the baseline.

The trade-off changes on YOLOS: GP5 cuts median queries from 290 to 47.5 and raises success from 59.1% to 60.8%, but increases median area from 8.32% to 14.79%.
